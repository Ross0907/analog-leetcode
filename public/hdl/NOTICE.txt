VeriSim — https://github.com/senolgulgonul/verisim
Acknowledged for its three-stage browser-port approach. The deployed compiler

VCDrom 1.6.0 — WaveDrom contributors (MIT)
https://github.com/wavedrom/vcdrom
  /hdl/viewer/LICENSE
  /hdl/viewer/package-provenance.json
The VCD parser WASM is the unmodified npm release. Its WebGL renderer retains
native digital waveform rendering with small AnaCode presentation hooks:
  /hdl/viewer/presentation-patch.json
  /hdl/viewer/source/vcdrom-1.6.0.js.gz (exact upstream bundle input)
  /hdl/viewer/source/patch-vcdrom.mjs (reproducible MIT presentation patch)
The wrapper uses the upstream file importer, supplies fixed signal/value
columns, formats the parsed samples, and can display digital bus magnitudes
as analog traces. It does not replace the parser or HDL simulation engine.
The upstream page's analytics are removed.
